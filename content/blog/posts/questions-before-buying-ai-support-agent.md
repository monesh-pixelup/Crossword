Picking an AI support agent is easy to get wrong. Ask these five questions first.

## 1. Where does it get its answers?

It should answer only from your help center, docs and order data, and say so when it doesn't know.

## 2. How does it hand off to a human?

The customer should never repeat themselves. The agent passes the whole conversation to your team.

## 3. Can it sound like you?

Tone, words to avoid, how formal to be. You should set these, not the vendor.

## 4. What does it cost when it works?

Per-resolution pricing can surprise you in a busy month. Ask for a worked example at your volume.

## 5. How will you know it's working?

Resolution rate, handoff rate and customer ratings, visible from day one.
