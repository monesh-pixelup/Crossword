CROSSWORD: CTA ENVELOPE ANIMATION DEMO

A working prototype of the envelope at the bottom of the Crossword homepage: the
envelope opens and the two cards slide out. It's built from the Figma layers, so it
looks like the final design.


OPEN IT
1. Unzip the folder (keep everything in it together).
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome or Safari).
   No internet or install needed.
3. Scroll down. The envelope opens once it comes into view.

preview.mp4 shows how it plays at the moment, at full speed and then slowed down.


THE EDITOR (left side)
Top: Replay, and play it at 1x, 1/2 or 1/4 speed.

Timeline: one coloured bar per step (seal, flap lifts, flap lands, letter, photo),
so you can see how the steps overlap. Drag across the bars to move through the
animation by hand. Click a step's name to jump to its settings.

Steps 1 to 5: each step folds open to show its settings, with a short note under
each one.
- Drag a slider, or type a number. When you let go, the envelope replays.
- "Feel" sets how a movement speeds up and slows down: pick a curve (Gentle,
  Medium, Strong, Overshoot...) and a direction (ends slowly, starts slowly, or
  both). The little graph shows the curve.
- A gold dot marks every value you've changed. The arrow next to it puts that one
  value back to the original.

Bottom: how many values you've changed, "Reset all", and "Copy values".

Your values are saved in this browser, so reloading keeps them. "Hide" tucks the
editor away; "Show controls" (bottom left) brings it back.


GOOD TO KNOW
- The open flap always starts the moment the closed flap finishes. That keeps the
  switch between the two flaps invisible. Leave "Match tip speed" on for a smooth
  swing, or turn it off to set the land time yourself.
- If a card's "Starts below" goes much lower than its original (letter 100%,
  photo 62%), the card shows in the opening before the flap lifts.
- Every curve in the "Feel" lists can be built in Webflow as-is.
- The desktop layout matches Figma. Below 768px wide is a proposal for phones.


SEND IT BACK
Click "Copy values" at the bottom of the editor and paste the result into your
reply. It holds all your settings and the steps needed for the Webflow build. If
the browser blocks copying, a box opens with the text selected: copy it from there.
